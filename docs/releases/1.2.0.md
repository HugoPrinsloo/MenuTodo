MenuTodo 1.2.0 adds time estimates and a progress bar for your list.

- Pick a time estimate for any todo (5m–4h) from the clock button at the end of the row, which appears while you hover over the list.
- A progress bar under the list title shows how much of your estimated time is done; todos without an estimate count as the average.
- The footer now shows the estimated time remaining, e.g. "3 left · ~1h 15m".
- Estimates stay on your Mac — they aren't synced to Reminders.
