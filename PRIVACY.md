# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-12_

MenuTodo is a menu bar to-do list app for macOS.

- **Your todos are stored locally on your device.** MenuTodo does not operate a server and does not have access to your list.
- **Optional Reminders sync.** If you connect a Reminders list, MenuTodo uses Apple's EventKit framework (with your permission) to keep your todos in two-way sync with that list. This data stays on your device and in your own iCloud account — it is never sent to the developer.
- **No analytics, no tracking.** MenuTodo does not collect usage data, does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.
- **No data is transmitted to the developer.** The developer has no access to your todos or any other data MenuTodo creates.

Questions about this policy can be sent to hugo8prinsloo@gmail.com.
